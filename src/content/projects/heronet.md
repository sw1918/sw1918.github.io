HeroNet is a game I've been developing since the summer of 2024. You play as an employee of HeroNet,
a company that solves people's random problems (generally through violence). It's a grid-based, tactical
roguelike, where you progress through different contracts, hiring heroes, buying items, and completing 
random requests from clients each battle.

The game includes 6 story-based contracts, 6 endless contracts, and an arcade mode, as well as over 100 
characters and nearly 3 hours of original music.
