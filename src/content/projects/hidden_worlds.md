Insofar as there's a story behind this album, it's probably a memory of the House Hippo PSA that ran in Canada when I was a kid. It was framed as 
a realistic description of the North American house hippo, meant to explain to kids not to believe everything they see on TV. However, I 
remember I DID believe in the house hippo, and remember looking in my house for one. Can you believe that stupid little boy grew up to be a 
teaching professor? Believe in yourself!

This album is probably my favorite music project I've done to date. Almost every song is based on specific memories from my childhood. For example, 
At the Prize Counter is inspired by an arcade we used to visit where all the worthwhile toys were worth like 10000000 tickets, so I'd go home with 
a single plastic army man or a sticky hand or something. Did You Know? is based on incidents like the House Hippo situation, where you'd just 
believe anything if it was said with confidence. 

The song with the most specific references is probably I'm a gamer, not because I have no life, but because I choose to have many. It's based on 
the many, many video games I played as a kid. In total, I think it references Super Mario 64, Super Mario Sunshine, Super Smash Bros. Melee, 
Naruto: Clash of Ninja 2, Lord of the Rings: Conquest, Age of Mythology, Sim City, The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask, Final Fantasy VI (three times), 
Tony Hawk's American Wasteland, Banjo Kazooie, Castle Crashers, The Simpsons: Bart's Nightmare, Roblox, Sonic the Hedgehog, Animal Crossing, 
Terraria, Maplestory, Mario Kart, and Chrono Trigger. 

It's also the only album I recorded with my banjo! I play it on three songs: Junkyard Song, Washed Away, and Hidden Worlds.
