The soundtrack I wrote for my game, HeroNet. For whatever reason, it took me forever to figure out the right vibe for this game; my FL Studio projects
folder is full of discarded 1-minute drafts. I think they started too ambient, like something you'd hear in an RTS, but they weren't right for the cartoony-corporate
vibe I was going for here. Bunnies Making Decisions was the first track I finally finished, and everything eventually flowed from there. 

I think my favorite run of tracks are the ones that play in the Inferno Suite contract (Bad Buns, Terrible Toys, Brutish Bugs...). Remixing some of the earlier
themes using the instruments that the musician squad plays (banjo, flute, trumpet, and violin) wound up being pretty fun, and I basically did one per day until
I was done with that section of the game. (The real peek behind the curtain is that I only used those instruments for the characters because they were easy to
design with pixel art for me; all things considered, I think they sound ok together).

Another note on the overall composition: ever since I was a teenager writing music for unfinished projects, I remember thinking the idea of each boss theme
being a remix of the level theme leading up to them was a cool idea, so I finally put it into practice here. I could have sworn there's a specific game that
gave me that idea, but I'm struggling to remember what it was. Donkey Kong 64 maybe? I feel like there's another one I'm forgetting, though. 

As a final aside, there are four tracks in this OST that weren't originally written for the game: Good Job, Stone Battle, Angel Battle, and Goop Factory. For 
a while I had a "Generic Video Game Music" folder where I'd throw things that I wrote for no specific reason, but felt like they should be a video game music
loop. So, if they sound slightly out-of-place, it's because I got lazy.
